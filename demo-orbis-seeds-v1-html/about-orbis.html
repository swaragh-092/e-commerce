About Orbi

Orbi Seeds International Pvt Ltd is a company with difference. We innovate and breed seeds that match the needs of the global farming community. We harness the best talent in vegetable breeding and consistently supply the best quality seeds to farmers at affordable prices. Hence our tagline reads "Talent for Breeding and Innovation".

Orbi has a strong R&D base in India, Thailand, and the USA. The stations in India and Thailand cater to breeding vegetable seeds for the Asian markets whereas, the station in the USA focuses on the American and European markets. In USA R&D we do produce and breed organic seeds suitable for the organic producers worldwide. The Bangalore station has a state of art molecular marker and DH facility. In addition, there are three R&D stations in Karnataka equipped with the latest breeding facilities. While recognizing the increasing interest in protected cultivation, the company has launched a strong program on protected cultivation that focuses on sweet peppers, other exotic peppers, melons, watermelons, tomatoes, cucumbers, jalapenos, etc.

Who we are

Orbi Seeds International Pvt. Ltd. is a company with a difference. Established in 2016 by Dr. Sanjay Dwivedi, a professional with more than 25 years of experience in the seed industry, Orbi was founded with a clear vision - to innovate, breed and deliver seeds that respond to the evolving needs of the global farming community.

Research is our strength

At Orbi Seeds, research and development is at the heart of everything we do.

Our R&D stations at Bengaluru in India, Hollister in the USA and Chiang Mai in Thailand focus on the requirements of different global markets.

India and Thailand focus primarily on Asian markets, while the USA station serves American and European markets and also develops and produces organic seeds to support organic farming.

Interview of Production Head

With advanced molecular marker, DH and other breeding facilities, Orbi combines modern science with practical breeding expertise.

Our strong R&D team works on superior hybrids and varieties across major crops including tomato, chilli, brinjal, okra, hot peppers, sweet peppers, melons, watermelons, maize, flowers, cotton, cucumber and gourds.

We also focus on developing product lines for worldwide markets, with special emphasis on processing markets and niche products of heritage importance.

Excellence in Seed Processing

To ensure quality from seed to market, Orbi Seeds has established a dedicated seed processing and packing facility, equipped with advanced systems for testing, sorting, grading, packing and storage—ensuring precision, consistency and quality at every stage. Our well-equipped molecular laboratory strengthens breeding programmes and helps develop new hybrids and combinations faster. A centralised nursery further supports seedling production, completing the journey from advanced breeding to healthy planting material.

Today, Orbi Seeds supplies high-quality seeds to major markets around the world, earning the trust of farmers and partners across the globe.

As a growing global seed brand, Orbi continues to innovate and develop new seed solutions in response to the changing needs of farming sector. Because the future of farming demands better seeds, better solutions and continuous innovation.
