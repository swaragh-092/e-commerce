About Orbi

Orbi Seeds International Pvt Ltd was formed by Dr. Sanjay Dwivedi in October 2016 as proprietor company and later in October 2018 it became a private limited company. The aim of forming this company was to give knowledge to the Indian & Asian farmers on how to do commercial processing tomato growing and effective cultivation to encourage processing industries in India. As the scope increased and ORBI Seeds started small scale seeds sale/ distribution operations in Bangalore and Thailand which later became a full-fledged Seed company with Research and Development, Production and Marketing teams getting added over the time.

Research & Development

01. Research On Tomato Seeds

ORBI SEEDS has focused considerable effort on breeding tomatoes and hot peppers for Asian and Middle Eastern markets. Over the past five to six years, the company has conducted extensive breeding programs, continually advancing seed material to develop hybrids for these regions as rapidly as possible.

Know More

02. Research On Cucumber Seeds

The cucumber breeding program has taken shape over the past year, with a focused effort aimed at the Indian, Asian, and pickling markets. Early-stage trials across India have already shown promise for products from last year's crosses, such as 2506 and 2507, which display good potential.

Know More

03. Research On Sweet Corn Seeds

Initiated in 2019 by Dr. Cheunjit, the sweet corn breeding program has led to the development of many hybrids suited for Asian and Middle Eastern markets. The program is presently focused on creating hybrids for semi-tropical and temperate climates, particularly targeting super sweet types. ORBI SEEDS expects to introduce these varieties to the market by 2027.
